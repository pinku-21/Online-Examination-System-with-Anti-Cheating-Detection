Deep Dive

Engineering Challenges & Solutions

Key technical hurdles encountered during development and how they were systematically engineered.

1

Custom YOLOv11 Computer Vision & Continuous 468-Landmark Biometric Embeddings

Custom Deep Learning

Challenge: Generic public models (like standard COCO) are trained on arbitrary general objects (chairs, cups, dogs) and produce false alarms or miss concealed smartphones, ear pieces, and cheat papers under erratic student room lighting. Furthermore, single check-in facial checks leave exams vulnerable to mid-test proxy student substitution.
Solution & Architecture:

  • Transfer Learning on YOLO11n: Fine-tuned Ultralytics YOLOv11 Nano on curated, annotated proctoring datasets over 50 epochs on Google Colab T4 GPU with photometric and geometric augmentations (random rotation, brightness shift, Gaussian blur).
  • WebGL Edge Quantization: Exported PyTorch weights (best.pt) to sharded TensorFlow.js graph model (model.json) executed on student GPUs via WebGL at zero server compute cost.
  • Continuous Biometric Embedding Similarity: Combined Google MediaPipe FaceMesh to extract 468 3D facial vectors ($E_{ ext{live}}$) and computed ongoing cosine similarity against registered enrollment vectors ($E_{ ext{ref}}$):
    Sim(E_ref, E_live) = (E_ref · E_live) / (||E_ref|| ||E_live||)
    If similarity drops below the calibrated 0.72 threshold, a biometric impostor violation (SEC-002) is immediately logged and locks the session.
2

Hybrid Cloud Storage & Network-Level VPN / Proxy Hazard Auditing

Infrastructure & Threat Intel

Challenge: Ephemeral server container reboots on cloud platforms (e.g., Render) cause uploaded student answer photos and forensic webcam violation captures to vanish. Additionally, remote test-takers use Tor and datacenter proxies to disguise geographic location or hire proxy exam proxies.
Solution: Architected persistent media streaming via Cloudinary CDN so multi-page handwritten math proofs and webcam forensic snaps survive server restarts and feed into Gemini Vision AI. Integrated BigDataCloud Hazard API and ipapi.co to cross-examine incoming Autonomous System Numbers (ASNs) against datacenter registries, flagging Tor exit nodes, proxies, and VPNs on the tutor audit dashboard.

1

Mathematical Notation & KaTeX Equation Rendering

Challenge: AI question generation often outputs plain strings like sqrt(16) or pi = 3.14, which confused students and led to option parsing issues when options contained alphanumeric labels.
Solution: Implemented automated prompt instructions forcing standard LaTeX delimiters ($$...$$, \(...\)) paired with client-side KaTeX auto-render engines on all question and option nodes, ensuring clean typesetting across desktop and mobile.

2

Eliminating False-Positive Penalties During Handwritten Uploads

Challenge: When students looked down at desk paper to write equations or held papers up to the camera, the TensorFlow.js model flagged candidate absence or face-hidden, penalizing honest test takers.
Solution: Designed a context-aware Upload Mode Penalty Shield. When the file upload dialog or webcam photo capture modal is actively engaged for subjective questions, proctoring penalties are gracefully suppressed while maintaining audit snapshot logging.

3

10-Second Inactivity / Cheating Standoff Auto-Submission

Challenge: If a student tabbed away or minimized the window, a strike was triggered. However, savvy students could leave the warning overlay active indefinitely while continuing to cheat on a secondary phone or split screen.
Solution: Introduced a recurrent countdown watchdog. If the student does not actively click "Return to Exam" within 10 seconds of a strike, subsequent strikes are automatically incremented every 10 seconds until Strike 10 triggers forced auto-submission.

4

Dual Multimodal AI Architecture with Failover

Challenge: Cloud AI rate limits (e.g. Gemini quota exhaustion during large classroom batches) could stall exam creation.
Solution: Built a unified routing layer that attempts Gemini 2.5 Flash first for multimodal vision & PDF extraction, and seamlessly falls back to Groq Llama-3.3 70B Versatile for high-speed question generation, with mock fallback as ultimate safeguard.

5

Cryptographic Non-Repudiation & Hardware Stream Anti-Spoofing

Challenge: Advanced students bypass webcam proctoring by feeding pre-recorded looping video via virtual camera drivers (OBS Virtual Cam, ManyCam), while attackers might try to intercept API traffic or edit local storage variables to reduce strike counts.
Solution: Implemented hardware driver inspection via navigator.mediaDevices.enumerateDevices() filtering out virtual device drivers, active client-side DevTools debugger latency traps, and server-side HMAC-SHA256 digital certificates guaranteeing database immutability and submission non-repudiation.
