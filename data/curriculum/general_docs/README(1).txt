SCIENCE & BIOLOGY DIAGRAM CSV DATASETS
======================================

Contains 8 topic CSVs:
1. Human digestive system
2. Human respiratory system
3. Human circulatory system
4. Structure of a flower
5. Plant cell
6. Animal cell
7. Nephron
8. Human brain

Also includes master_science_biology_diagrams.csv combining all records.

Each CSV provides structures/parts, a short main function, and a sample exam prompt.
These are label/reference datasets, not diagram images. Use them to make blank-label
worksheets and teacher answer keys. Exact diagrams and the complexity of labels vary
by class and board; check the relevant CBSE/ICSE syllabus and textbook.
For accuracy, use textbook diagrams when drawing the structures, particularly for
nephron, brain, and circulatory-system vessels.
